## Usage Rules

1. Use bold for: headings, key phrases, names, numbers that matter
2. Use italic for: quotes, emphasis on single words, subtle highlights
3. Don't overuse — if everything is bold, nothing is bold
4. Keep bold sections short (2–5 words max)
5. Numbers and punctuation don't have Unicode bold/italic — leave them normal
